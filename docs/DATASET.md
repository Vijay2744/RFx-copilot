# The demo dataset

Generated by `python data/generate_dataset.py` (seeded, so it's reproducible). Category: **corrugated
packaging**, chosen because its units are naturally messy: per piece, per 100, per kg; 3, 5 and 7-ply; board
weight varies by size.

## The RFx: `data/rfx.json`

- **RFQ-PKG-2026-114**: annual rate contract for Acme Consumer Goods, Hosur plant
- **30 lines**: 12 × 3-ply (RSC boxes, die-cut mailers, partitions), 15 × 5-ply (boxes, pad, tray),
  3 × 7-ply heavy-duty boxes; each with dimensions, nominal weight (kg/pc) and annual quantity
- **≈ ₹4 crore** annual value at market prices
- **Requested basis:** ₹ per piece, ex-GST, delivered to Hosur
- **Questionnaire (5):** ISO 9001 with certificate · batch BCT/ECT reports · capacity · lead time · 60-day payment
- **Quality rule:** valid (unexpired) ISO certificate AND batch test reports

## Last year's contract: `data/last_year_contract.csv`

The incumbent (Om Packaging) at last year's prices, needed to resolve "rest same as last year".

## The five replies: `data/vendor_responses/`

| Folder | Files | Deliberate traps |
|---|---|---|
| `V1_shree_balaji` | `Shree_Balaji_Quotation.xlsx` (Quotation + Compliance sheets) | Ignores our template; own item codes; shuffled order; **prices include 18% GST**; **decimal typo on L12** (10× too low); 45-day credit only; ISO claimed with no certificate attached |
| `V2_ganesh` | `Ganesh_Corrugators_Offer.pdf`, `Ganesh_ISO9001_Certificate.pdf` | Letterhead PDF; **4% discount only in a footnote, conditional on PO > ₹50 L**; **offers 5-ply in place of 7-ply on L28**; questionnaire on page 2; 90-day validity vs 12 months asked |
| `V3_packworld` | `PackWorld_Proposal.docx`, `PackWorld_ISO_Certificate.pdf` | Commercials in paragraphs; **prices in USD, FOB Chennai port** (freight extra); cheapest on paper; **claims ISO 9001 but the attached certificate expired 14-Aug-2025**; batch tests "on request at extra cost"; won't accept 60 days |
| `V4_sai_krupa` | `SaiKrupa_RateCard_photo.jpg`, `whatsapp_message.txt` | **Angled, unevenly lit phone photo**; generic rate card; **prices per 100 pcs**; **27 of 30 lines** (no 7-ply); **no questionnaire** |
| `V5_om_packaging` | `email.eml`, `Om_ISO9001.pdf` | The incumbent's one-liner: **"₹42/kg for the 5-ply, 38 for the 3-ply, rest same as last year, freight extra"**: per-kg pricing, a rule instead of numbers, freight with no amount, and a possible double charge (last year's prices were delivered) |

## `data/_ground_truth_for_tests.json`

What each vendor *meant* to charge per piece, ex-GST. **The app never reads it.** It exists only so tests can
check that extraction plus normalization recovers the truth.
